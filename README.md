# andMoney

A faithful recreation of the classic **anMoney** personal finance app for modern Android, rebuilt from the ground up using current Android architecture, Jetpack components, and clean design patterns.

---

## 📌 Background

For many longtime users, the original **anMoney** was the gold standard of Android personal finance management: fast, deeply functional, and privacy-focused with local data storage. However, as Android evolved, the original app ceased active development and struggled with modern device compatibility and OS standards.

**andMoney** exists to bridge that gap. The goal is simple: capture the workflow, depth, and familiarity of the original anMoney while leveraging modern Android architecture, improved reliability, and updated Material design.

## 💬 How to Contribute & Provide Feedback

To keep development organized and maintainable, please direct your input to the right place:

### 💡 Feature Requests & Ideas → [Discussions](../../discussions)
Have an idea for a new feature, UI tweak, or workflow improvement? 
* Please share it in **[GitHub Discussions](../../discussions)** under the *Ideas* category.
* **Please do not open an Issue for a feature request.**

### 🐛 Bug Reports → [Issues](../../issues)
Found something broken or behaving unexpectedly?
* Search the **[existing issues](../../issues)** first to confirm it hasn't already been reported.
* If it's new, open a **[New Issue](../../issues/new)** and provide:
  * Android version and device model
  * Steps to reproduce the bug
  * Expected vs. actual behavior
  * Screenshots (if applicable and not showing private data)

## 🔒 Privacy & Local Storage

Like its predecessor, **andMoney** respects your financial privacy:
* All data is stored locally on your device.
* No third-party tracking, analytics, or remote harvesting of your transactions.
